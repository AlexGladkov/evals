### EN 🇬🇧

**Mining Monitor for NiceHash** gives you a fast, read-only dashboard for your NiceHash mining rigs — right on your phone. No login required: just enter your BTC mining address and get instant access to profitability, balance, payouts and workers.

**WHAT YOU CAN MONITOR:**

💰 **Profitability (Income)**
- Real-time earnings: Hour, Day, Week, Month
- BTC and 15+ fiat currencies (USD, EUR, RUB, GBP, AUD …)

⚖️ **Balance by Algorithm**
- See unpaid balance and daily income per algorithm
- Hashrate per algorithm with live speed

💸 **Payouts**
- Full payout history with timestamps
- Fee breakdown per transaction

🖥️ **Workers**
- Online/offline status per worker
- Hashrate, difficulty, uptime per rig
- Grouped by location

📊 **Charts**
- Hashrate trends: 1h, 6h, 12h, 1d, 3d, 1w

🎨 **Themes**
- Multiple color themes, including Dark mode

🌍 **Multi-language**
- English, Russian, Chinese
